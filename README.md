# Restaurant Sales Performance Analysis | Excel

An independent Excel data analysis project focused on restaurant sales, profitability, and business performance.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes restaurant sales data from raw data cleaning and validation through business analysis and the creation of an interactive Excel dashboard.

The analysis focuses on performance by Branch, Sales Channel, Employee, and Menu Item, with a comparison between Actual Margin and Target Margin.

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning & Validation
- XLOOKUP
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Conditional Formatting
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard
- Slicers

## Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: **1,287.50 JOD**
- Total Cost: **808.05 JOD**
- Total Profit: **479.45 JOD**
- Average Order Value: **14.31 JOD**
- Actual Profit Margin: **37.24%**
- Target Margin: **37.40%**

## Key Insights

- Amman generated the highest Revenue and Profit.
- Irbid achieved the highest Profit Margin.
- Dine-in was the strongest Sales Channel by Revenue and Profit.
- Omar was the highest-performing Employee by Revenue and Profit.
- Beef Burger, Chicken Burger, and Alfredo Pasta were below their Target Margin.
- Beef Burger had the largest negative Margin Gap.

## Recommendations

- Review costs and pricing for the Burger category, especially Beef Burger and Chicken Burger, to understand why margins are below target.
- Analyze the Zarqa branch to identify the reasons behind its lower Revenue and Profit compared with the other branches.

## Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- KPI Cards
- Revenue & Profit by Branch
- Revenue & Profit by Channel
- Revenue & Profit by Employee
- Actual vs Target Margin by Item
- Interactive Slicers connected to charts and KPIs
